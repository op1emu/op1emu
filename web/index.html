op1emu

An emulator of the Teenage Engineering OP-1 that runs its own firmware, here in the browser. It needs the firmware as your OP-1 has it: its NAND image (528 MiB) and its otp.bin (8 KiB). They stay in this browser, in its private storage for this site, and are not uploaded anywhere. The BF526's boot ROM is fetched once from op1emu/bfin_sim. The guest's writes go to the stored image, as to the device's flash.

Controls

Click the keys; hold one with the mouse or a finger. Click either half of an encoder or the volume knob, or scroll over it, to turn it. On the computer's keyboard the musical keys are Q…[ and Z X C (white), 2 3 4 6 7 9 0 - S D (black), and Shift is shift.

It needs Chrome or Edge 133 or later (Firefox runs out of room for the compiled code). A boot takes about a minute on a fast computer, every time: the emulator compiles the firmware's code as it runs it. ?audio=MS sets the audio buffer (default 100 ms).
