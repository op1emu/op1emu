op1emu

An emulator of the Teenage Engineering OP-1 that runs its own firmware, here in the browser. For manual boot, choose your OP-1's NAND image (528 MiB) and matching otp.bin (8 KiB), or use the experimental firmware preparation below. Your inputs stay in this browser and are not uploaded anywhere. The BF526's boot ROM is fetched once from op1emu/bfin_sim. The guest's writes go to the stored image, as to the device's flash.

Hold COM to start in te-boot; release to let go of the key.

Prepare from firmware (experimental, original OP-1 version 246)

Choose your original .op1 update instead of preparing a NAND image manually. Decryption and factory-content provisioning happen locally. This creates a new 528 MiB NAND image and plaintext loader, like the desktop direct-LDR workflow. Only successful preparation replaces the active boot configuration. Start, Export, and the labels below always use that same configuration.

Supply your own OTP text containing the decryption-key pages, which you are authorized to use. This is not the complete 8 KiB otp.bin used by manual NAND boot. No decryption material is downloaded or cached. Prepared firmware uses the emulator's generated default chip identity, as a fresh desktop direct-LDR session does.

Controls

Click the keys; hold one with the mouse or a finger. Click either half of an encoder or the volume knob, or scroll over it, to turn it. On the computer's keyboard the musical keys are Q…[ and Z X C (white), 2 3 4 6 7 9 0 - S D (black), and Shift is shift.

It needs Chrome or Edge 133 or later (Firefox runs out of room for the compiled code). A boot takes about a minute on a fast computer, every time: the emulator compiles the firmware's code as it runs it. ?audio=MS sets the audio buffer (default 100 ms).
